## Full description (max 4000 chars)

TapSense shows you exactly where your phone's NFC antenna is, so you never fumble again when tapping to pay, unlocking a smart lock, boarding transit, or badging into the office.

Can't find the NFC chip location on your phone? Wondering where the NFC antenna is on your Samsung, Pixel, OnePlus, or Xiaomi device. TapSense finds it for you - no guessing, no digging through support forums.

KEY FEATURES

- Auto-detects the phone you're holding - no setup required.
- Precise NFC antenna location on supported Android 14+ devices, using real on-device antenna data - not estimates.
- A growing database of verified device antenna locations, with vendor-confirmed models marked as exact.
- Clear guidance even on phones we don't recognize yet, via a guided sweep animation that helps you locate your NFC reader zone.
- A real tap test confirms your tap zone actually works with a live NFC tag, card, or reader - not just a guess.
- Preview the NFC tap zone for a different phone before you switch or upgrade.
- 100% offline - no internet connection, account, or sign-up required.

WHERE TAPSENSE HELPS

- Contactless payments: Google Pay, Google Wallet, Samsung Pay, and tap-to-pay at checkout.
- Public transit: tapping your phone on subway, bus, or train NFC readers.
- Smart locks & access control: hotel key cards, office badges, and NFC door locks.
- NFC tags: reading and writing NFC stickers, tags, and cards without hunting for the right spot.
- Troubleshooting "NFC not working": often the real problem is tapping the wrong spot - TapSense fixes that instantly.

HONEST ACCURACY, NOT GUESSWORK
We never claim 100% accuracy for every device. Instead, every result is labeled Exact, Approximate, Estimated, or Unknown, so you always know whether you're looking at a verified measurement or a best-effort guess.

PRIVACY FIRST
No account required. No personal data collected. All device and antenna-position data stays on your device.

Whether you carry a Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, OnePlus, Xiaomi, or another Android phone, TapSense is the fastest way to find your NFC antenna, NFC sensor, or NFC chip location - and finally tap with confidence.
